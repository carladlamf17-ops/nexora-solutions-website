BEFORE WE TALK

Can you connect my business modules?+

Yes. We can define a system connecting accounting, administration, inventory, sales and operations, or start with one module. We review data, responsibilities and integrations before agreeing on scope.

Can the system fit my business?+

Yes. We review the workflow first, then define the functions. Our portfolio shows different uses of custom software.

Can it run on the web, Windows or mobile?+

We develop for these platforms. Together we choose where your system needs to run, based on your devices and workflow.

How is the budget defined?+

By scope: functions, platforms, integrations and stages. Write to us about your needs so we can review them.
