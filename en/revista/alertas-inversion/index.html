Investment alerts in Venezuela: what was published and when

A current warning and a historical case answer different questions. Check the date, source, and scope before sharing a name.

Timeline

  • 2024 · SolesBot: Unión Radio reported that operations stopped after complaints.
  • 2024 · Hyper AI: BeInCrypto reported users’ allegations of a suspected Ponzi.

What the sources document

Venevisión reported the October 30, 2026 Sunaval warning about offers circulating on WhatsApp and Telegram. The other sources document past cases and do not establish current recruitment.

What to do, step by step

  1. Check the date and original link before forwarding an allegation.
  2. Confirm the legal name and exact domain. Similar names may belong to different sites.
  3. Contact the regulator independently when an offer claims authorization.
