01 / THE NEED

Understand exposure and set priorities.

We start with the services, devices and applications the business uses. With their owners, we review information, access needs and operationally critical points. This context defines review scope and improvements to assess.

02 / SECURITY REVIEW

What we review.

  • Access, account and permissions review.
  • Network configuration and service exposure assessment.
  • Application and information protection practices review.
  • Recommendations and priorities for strengthening security.

03 / REVIEW SCOPE

How we define the review.

We agree with the business on authorised systems, review type, work windows and deliverables. Any infrastructure or application testing requires explicit scope and authorisation before execution.
