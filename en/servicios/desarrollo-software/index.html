01 / THE NEED

A system that follows your workflow.

We build custom administrative, accounting, commercial and operational systems, as well as applications for industry workflows. The starting point can be a specific need or several departments that need to work together. We review who records information, who approves it and who needs to read it; that workflow defines the project screens, functions and integrations.

02 / FUNCTIONS

Modules and functions.

  • Customer, document and operations records and search.
  • Roles and permissions based on team responsibilities.
  • Reports and search for reviewing workflow information.
  • Web, Windows or mobile platform chosen around available devices.

03 / PROJECT SCOPE

What we agree before development.

Before proposing a solution, we review existing tools, user numbers, internet access and required integrations. Data migration, offline operation, training and support are included only when agreed in the scope.

Nexora is based in El Tigre, Anzoátegui. We review projects for businesses in Venezuela and agree on meetings, delivery and support arrangements for each project.
