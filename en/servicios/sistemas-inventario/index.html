We develop inventory systems for businesses in Venezuela from El Tigre, Anzoátegui. We connect product and movement records with the stock information your team needs.

Discuss my project ↗

01 / YOUR WORKFLOW

Know what is in stock and what has moved.

A shop may need to search by reference, receive goods and record issues. The warehouse also needs to count physical stock and review differences. We define these workflows with you so the system supports office and warehouse work.

02 / POSSIBLE SCOPE

What your team needs.

  • Product catalogue and search using agreed fields.
  • Goods receipts, stock issues and movement enquiries.
  • Physical stock counts and discrepancy review following the defined process.
  • Stock enquiries and reports for authorised staff.

03 / BEFORE DEVELOPMENT

Define the scope together.

We review product references, units, warehouses and documents before defining the system. If you need barcodes, mobile counts, quotations, valuation or accounting integration, we assess available data and devices and detail the work in the proposal.

Nexora is based in El Tigre, Anzoátegui. We review projects for businesses in Venezuela and agree on meetings, delivery and support arrangements for each project.

Before we talk.

Can you build a system for a hardware business?+

Yes. The portfolio includes Ferrearca, with inventory, goods receipts, issues and physical stock counts. Its functions are a reference; your system scope follows your own workflows.

Can you import my inventory from Excel?+

We first review structure, references, units and data quality. Importing and validating opening stock must be agreed as part of the project.

Can I count products using a phone?+

The Ferrearca portfolio includes Android stock counting. For your business, we review devices, connectivity and product identification before defining a mobile solution.
